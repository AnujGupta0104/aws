About

Virat Kohli (born 5 November 1988) is one of the leading batsmen of his generation. Known for his technique, consistency and intensity, Kohli has captained India across formats and earned numerous international and domestic honours.

Career summary

Kohli's international career began in August 2008 after a surprise call-up to the ODI squad. He rose quickly through the ranks, demonstrating remarkable adaptability across formats and contributing heavily to India's major tournament victories.
